How we say thank you

  • ★ Ambassador membership for you and your spouse or partner: every Ambassador benefit, with full voting rights
  • Your name, logo and link on our Sponsors page while you sponsor
  • A thank-you from the podium at club meetings and forums
  • Your name and logo on our thank-you sign at events and in printed programs
  • ★ Promotion: six reserved seats at our Inaugural Gala (one-time, annual sponsorships only)
  • A thank-you post on our Instagram, X, Facebook and YouTube

Who becomes a member. The sponsorship includes Ambassador membership, with voting rights, for the person who pays it (the principal) and one spouse or partner. It does not make anyone else at the business (other owners, employees or members) part of the club; they can join on their own.

A thank-you, not a marketing service. Sponsors receive a thank-you acknowledgment of their support. The club does not sell advertising, marketing services, endorsements or comparisons with other businesses.
